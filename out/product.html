Product & User Experience

Your Soul-Injected AI Partner — Powered by OpenClaw & NemoClaw

7.1 Personal Agent

The Personal Agent is twin3's user-facing product. It pairs each user's Twin Matrix digital twin with an AI agent, creating a unified, soul-injected companion. Built on the OpenClaw and Nvidia NemoClaw frameworks, the Personal Agent combines open-source flexibility with enterprise-grade security — delivering autonomous capabilities that remain fully under user control.

FEATURE 01

Identity Dashboard

Visualize and manage your 256D Twin Matrix. Track DNAs across all four quadrants. Watch your digital soul evolve.

FEATURE 02

Agent Manager

Deploy, configure, and monitor multiple Personal Agents. Set scopes, authorization levels, and validity periods.

FEATURE 03

Task Engine

Receive, accept, and execute HaaS tasks matched to your profile. Track earnings, completion rates, and reputation.

FEATURE 04

Element Market

Browse and acquire verified experience elements. Trade authenticated human data assets within the ecosystem.

FEATURE 05

Wallet Integration

Connect via Phantom, Rainbow, MetaMask, or WalletConnect. Full Web3-native with fiat onramps for Web2 users.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

7.2 Design Philosophy: Empowerment Over Extraction

01

Users own their data

The Twin Matrix SBT belongs to the user. twin3 cannot access, sell, or share it without explicit authorization.

02

Users control their agents

All agent actions are scoped and revocable. The user is always in charge.

03

Users capture majority value

70% of marketplace value flows directly to contributors, not the platform.
